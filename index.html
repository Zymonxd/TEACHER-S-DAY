Dear Teacher,

Today, we celebrate you—not only for the lessons you teach, but for the patience, encouragement, and kindness you share every day.

We may not always say it, but we truly appreciate everything you do.

Thank you for being part of our journey, for believing in us, and for helping us become better versions of ourselves.

Happy Teacher's Day!
With gratitude,
